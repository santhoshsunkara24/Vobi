Foundations

Blue

Accent only. 600 is the primary in light mode, 500 in dark. 50–100 are for soft fills, 300 for focus and borders.

Meet Vobi

A small blob that lives in a pill at the bottom of your screen. It breathes when idle, wobbles with your voice, thinks while it polishes, and does a tiny hop when your text lands.

Idle

Small and calm. Slow breathing, a blink every few seconds. Can be hidden.

Listening

The pill opens. Vobi stretches and wobbles with your voice; bars show the level.

Thinking

The text is being polished. Vobi’s eyes glance around; the label shimmers.

Done

A happy hop and ^‿^ eyes. “Pasted” for a moment, then it tucks away.

Error

A small headshake, with the problem in plain words. Never a scary dialog.

The app

You rarely open it, because Vobi works from anywhere. When you do, it’s calm and quick: what you said, the words it should know, and a few settings.
